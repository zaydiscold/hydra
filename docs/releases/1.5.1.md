# Hydra 1.5.1

Date: 2026-06-02
Baseline: `v1.5.0`

## Summary

Hydra `1.5.1` is an Account Generator rescue patch for signup flows that could
look stuck while the upstream page was asking for verification or showing a
shifted OTP form.

## Changes

- Detects browser-side human verification promptly instead of waiting for a full
  OTP timeout first.
- Reports `manual_verification` when the isolated browser is blocked by an
  upstream security gate.
- Broadens OTP detection to segmented Clerk fields, single six-digit fields,
  numeric input-mode fields, and one-time-code inputs.
- Fills and submits both segmented and single-field OTP layouts.
- Submits Generator OTP without throwing the global loading veil over the app.
- Cleans up Generator copy and replaces the old bracketed verify label with a
  normal **Submit code** button.

## Notes

Hydra still leaves upstream human verification and email-code ownership with the
operator.
